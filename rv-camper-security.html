Common Questions

Does this work when camping with no cell service?

Yes! Pro Wifi Camera creates a direct peer-to-peer stream across your local Wi-Fi network. As long as both devices connect to the same Wi-Fi router (even a compact 5V travel router with no WAN internet cable), streaming works flawlessly.

Will this drain my RV house battery?

No. The app includes an ultra-efficient black-screen power-saving mode that turns the screen off while keeping the camera sensor streaming. A standard 10,000mAh portable USB power bank can easily power the camera phone for an entire day of boondocking.

Can I use this as an RV backup or hitching camera?

Yes! Many solo travelers mount an old phone on their rear bumper or hitch using a magnetic mount, then view the low-latency video feed on their cab phone while backing up into tight campsites.

Is there a recurring subscription?

Never. Avoid expensive camper security subscriptions. For a one-time price of just $1.99, you get unlimited installs across your own Android devices.
