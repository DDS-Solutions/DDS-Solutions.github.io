Does Pro Wifi Camera Remote require an active internet connection?

No. It works entirely over your local Wi-Fi router or a phone hotspot. No internet access, account, or cloud service is required.

Can I view cameras remotely over the internet like Alfred?

No — and that is intentional. Camera and viewer must share the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. If you need internet remote viewing, Alfred (or a similar cloud cam) is a better fit. If you want private same-network monitoring without cloud or subscriptions, we are built for that.

Is there a monthly fee or are there ads?

Never. You pay $1.99 once on Google Play. No subscriptions, no ads, and no per-device fees — unlimited installs on compatible Android devices as described on our site.

What Android version do I need?

Android 12 or newer. Many people reuse older phones they already own as dedicated cameras.

Can I use multiple cameras at once?

Yes. Each Android device running the app can act as an independent local camera. Switch between feeds from your viewer on the same network — no extra per-camera subscription.
