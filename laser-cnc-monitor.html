Common Questions

Can I watch the stream on my PC while running LightBurn or CNC software?

Yes! Pro Wifi Camera features a built-in browser viewing mode. You can snap a browser window beside your laser or CNC controller (LightBurn, g-code senders, Fusion 360) and keep an eye on machine progress without needing another Android screen.

Why is low latency so important for laser engravers and CNC routers?

Cloud-based security cameras introduce delays of 3 to 10 seconds. When cutting acrylic or wood, a laser flare-up or CNC endmill bind can escalate quickly. Pro Wifi Camera streams directly over your local network with sub-second latency so you can hit the e-stop immediately.

Can I remotely light up dark machine enclosures?

Yes, you can toggle the phone's LED flashlight directly from the monitor interface or browser viewer, making it easy to check tool clearance and focus inside darkened enclosures.

Does it require an internet connection?

No internet is required. The video travels 100% locally over your workshop Wi-Fi network, ensuring complete privacy and reliable operation even during internet outages.
